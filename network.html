US Footy Network is a mentorship platform built for serious players. We connect high school and college-bound soccer athletes with current and former college players, and eventually professionals, who have been through it all. Recruiting decisions, film analysis, or just someone in your corner. The Network has you covered.

What the Network Offers

Three pillars, one goal.

Real guidance from people who have walked the path. Built around the three things that matter most for the jump from youth to college soccer.

Career Decisions

Navigate what level is right for you, which programs fit your profile, and how to position yourself for the best opportunity.

Player Analysis & Development

Get your film reviewed and receive real, position-specific feedback from players who have been through it.

Mentorship

Build confidence, develop the right mindset, and get guidance from mentors who understand the journey firsthand.
